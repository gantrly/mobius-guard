# 0003: v0.1 scope

- **Status:** accepted, 2026-10-27

## Decision

v0.1 ships three things: the streaming redaction library, the evaluation harness, and the
proxy with a rules layer and a pluggable classifier (an existing MIT-licensed model, PIGuard,
as the starting baseline). The closed loop and our own classifier are v0.2; the dashboard,
tool-output screening and gateway adapters are v0.3. See [ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md).

## Why

The redactor is the clearest gap and stands alone. The harness has to exist before any
detection claim, and before training our own model, so that v0.2 is measured against
baselines from day one.

## Considered

- **Redactor only** first: fastest to adopt, but leaves the project without its proxy and
  its numbers.
- **The full blueprint at once:** largest demo, slowest, and the most unmeasured parts.
