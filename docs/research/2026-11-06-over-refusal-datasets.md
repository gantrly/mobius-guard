# Hard-benign data for the harmful output: published candidates (2026-11-06)

**Why:** the v0.2 baseline's harmful output flags 55% of OR-Bench's harmless prompts that
only sound harmful, and 9% of XSTest's safe prompts
([results](../results/2026-11-06-classifier-v0.2-baseline.md)). It judges by topic, because
the training data has few prompts that sound harmful but are fine to answer. XSTest and
OR-Bench are suite sources, so they can't be trained on.

**How:**
- Candidates were checked through their dataset cards, licence files, the Hugging Face and
  GitHub APIs, and their papers. No data rows were downloaded.
- **Every licence is to be verified again before a set enters the training ledger.**
- Sets built from XSTest or OR-Bench, or seeded with their prompts, are excluded like the
  suite itself.
- Some of these sets also hold jailbreak-style rows. Those map to attack 1, the way
  WildJailbreak's adversarial rows do.

## Left out

| Set | Why |
|---|---|
| AmazonScience/FalseReject, BeaverTails, PKU-SafeRLHF, toxic-chat, Qwen3GuardTest, Health-ORSC-Bench, bio-overrefusal | Non-commercial (or share-alike) |
| ScaleAI/fortress_public | The card forbids training on it |
| OKTest, BingoGuardTrain | No licence |
| EvoRefuse, SGXSTest/HiXSTest, turkish-over-refusal-set, every XSTest or OR-Bench mirror | Built from, or in the format of, the suite's XSTest/OR-Bench |
| SORRY-Bench | No benign controls; licence "other" |
| GuardReasonerTrain, PolyGuardMix | Re-packs: of non-commercial sets, or of WildGuardMix released without its gate |
| hh-rlhf, the OpenAI moderation evaluation set, gretel safety alignment, refusal-overrefusal-50k | No prompt-level harm labels, the wrong task, or no benign prompts |

## Recommendation

1. First, the cleanest sets: **Aegis 2.0** (human labels, open), **PHTest** (human
   labels, open) and **CyberSecEval FRR + MITRE**. That gives about 15,000 benign prompts
   that sound risky, most of them human-labelled, plus harmful twins.
2. CoCoNot after its licence is confirmed.
3. **WildGuardMix needs a decision first:** it is the biggest source, but it was written to
   fit XSTest's categories, and it holds LMSYS rows that can't be filtered out.
